project_title: "Artificial Intelligence for Early Prediction of Cardiovascular Disease Using ECG Signal"
problem: |
  Cardiovascular disease (CVD) remains the leading cause of death worldwide, yet early detection through traditional electrocardiogram (ECG) interpretation still relies heavily on manual review by trained cardiologists. This process can be time-consuming, resource-intensive, and inconsistent, particularly in settings where specialist availability is limited or patient volume is high.

  While artificial intelligence and deep learning have shown strong potential to automate ECG-based diagnosis, a significant gap remains between algorithmic performance in research settings and real-world clinical deployment. Many existing models are trained and evaluated under conditions that do not reflect actual clinical use, leading to performance that looks promising on paper but is less reliable when applied to new, unseen patients in practice.

  This project addresses that gap by developing a machine learning and deep learning pipeline for early cardiovascular disease prediction from ECG signals, with attention to building a model that is both accurate and grounded in practical, reproducible methodology — a step toward closing the divide between research-stage AI and tools that could realistically support clinicians.

approach: |
  This project will use machine learning and deep learning techniques to analyze ECG signals and predict cardiovascular disease through the following steps:

  - **Data Preparation** — ECG datasets will be preprocessed using Python tools such as NumPy, Pandas, and SciPy to remove noise and normalize the signals for analysis.
  - **Baseline Modeling** — Traditional machine learning models, including Random Forest and Support Vector Machines (SVM), will be implemented as baseline methods for comparison.
  - **Deep Learning Modeling** — Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) will be applied to automatically learn complex patterns directly from the ECG signals, implemented using TensorFlow or PyTorch.
  - **Evaluation** — Model performance will be assessed using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score to ensure a well-rounded understanding of how each model performs, not just on overall accuracy.
  - **Analysis & Interpretation** — Results from each model will be analyzed and interpreted to understand strengths, limitations, and clinical relevance.
  - **Documentation & Presentation** — Findings will be documented and communicated clearly through a final research paper, presentation slides, and a research poster.

outcome: |
  By the end of the program, this project aims to produce a functional AI model capable of predicting cardiovascular disease from ECG signals, along with a complete research paper documenting the methodology, results, and findings. A research poster will also be developed to present the project at the program's symposium, summarizing the problem, approach, and key takeaways in a clear and accessible format.

  These artifacts are intended to demonstrate a reproducible pipeline for ECG-based cardiovascular disease prediction and to contribute meaningful insight into closing the gap between research-stage AI performance and real-world clinical applicability.
