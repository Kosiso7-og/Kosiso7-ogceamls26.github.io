what_i_learned: |
  The schedule was productive today, we kicked off with a Figma 101 session where we reviewed the requirements for our upcoming assignment due on June 3rd. It was also really inspiring to see some of the beautiful work my fellow participants have created so far and it motivated me to get started on my own assignment.
  After that, we had our research lab session where our faculty mentor walked us through the manual we will be using for our research project. He covered several important topics related to our research, discussed the progress made in previous related studies, and outlined the improvements we plan to incorporate in our own work. We also began downloading some of the datasets we will be working with — they were very large files, which gave me a sense of the scale of data we will be handling.
  Following the lunch break, we participated in an AI Quest activity, which was one of my favorite sessions for the day. It was incredibly fun and interactive, and I learned a great deal about how AI is being used to make meaningful positive changes in people's daily lives. The activity also emphasized the critical importance of properly researching, testing, and checking the accuracy of AI implementations before deploying them in real-world situations or on real people. The animated characters made the experience even more enjoyable and engaging. The key insight I am taking from the AI Quest into my own research is the importance of collecting diverse datasets from different demographics and varying constraints, all related to the same research problem, as well as the value of data cleaning to remove irrelevant or incorrect information and reduce error. This connects directly to what we have been reading in our literature review about the limitations of AI models trained on narrow datasets.
  We wrapped up the day with a Python 102 session where we continued learning about Gradio and explored some new features. We also created API keys, integrated them into Google Colab, and used them to implement AI chatbots directly within Gradio. It was a very informative and hands-on session, and we finished with a Kahoot to review everything we had learned about Gradio so far.

blockers: |

reflection: |

Every session added something different — from the creative inspiration of seeing my peers' Figma work, to the depth of the research lab discussion, to the fun and meaningful lessons from the AI Quest. The connection between what I learned in the AI Quest about diverse datasets and data cleaning and what I have been studying in my literature review made everything feel very cohesive. I just need to make sure I carve out time for that Figma assignment!
