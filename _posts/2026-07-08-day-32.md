what_i_learned: |
    Today began with continuing my literature review by reading additional research papers related to our project. As I reviewed more papers, I gained a better understanding of how researchers structure their introductions, support their claims with evidence, and motivate the significance of their work. I also began editing the introduction section of our research paper in Overleaf, incorporating feedback and refining the overall flow of the content. During the morning, our graduate mentor provided a refresher on how to properly manage references and create in-text citations in Overleaf, which was helpful as we continue expanding the bibliography for our paper. Learning these best practices will make collaborating on the paper much more efficient and ensure our citations remain organized and consistent.
    After lunch, we participated in SAIRI Connect, a session that I had been looking forward to. We started with an interactive game led by Joaquin where everyone partnered up and exchanged names. The challenge was to remember and respond using our partner's name instead of our own while quickly calling on another participant. Although the game was simple in concept, it quickly became surprisingly challenging because everyone instinctively responded to their own names. It was both confusing and hilarious, and despite eventually losing, it was a great team-building activity that encouraged us to think quickly and communicate under pressure.
    We ended the afternoon by watching the movie *Mercy*, which explored the growing role of artificial intelligence in society. I found the film especially interesting because it portrayed AI as an objective and unbiased decision-maker capable of evaluating situations purely through logic and data. At the same time, the movie highlighted an important limitation of artificial intelligence: unlike humans, AI lacks intuition, empathy, and the ability to interpret situations beyond the information it is given. Watching the film prompted me to think more deeply about both the potential and the limitations of AI, particularly as someone pursuing a career in computer science. It also made me interested in watching more science fiction films that explore similar themes.

blockers: |
    I did not encounter any major blockers today. Editing the introduction and managing references became much easier after the refresher on Overleaf citations, allowing me to continue making steady progress on the research paper.

reflection: |
    Today's activities provided a refreshing balance between research and personal development. Continuing to improve the introduction reminded me that writing a strong research paper is an iterative process that requires constant revision, careful attention to detail, and support from credible sources. The refresher on citations also reinforced the importance of proper academic writing practices, which are essential for producing high-quality research.
    SAIRI Connect was a welcome break from the technical work. The team-building game reminded me how easily our habits can influence our reactions, while the movie *Mercy* encouraged me to think critically about the future of artificial intelligence and its role in society. Although AI continues to advance rapidly, today's discussion reinforced my belief that qualities such as human intuition, empathy, and ethical judgment remain invaluable. Overall, the day was both productive and enjoyable, combining academic progress with meaningful conversations about technology and the future.
