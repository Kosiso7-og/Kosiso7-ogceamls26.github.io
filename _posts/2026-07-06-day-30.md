what_i_learned: |
    Today's research lab was focused entirely on advancing our research paper. My primary responsibility was writing the introduction section, which serves as the foundation for the rest of the paper. I spent the session researching relevant background information, organizing the overall flow of the introduction, and drafting the content in a way that clearly establishes the motivation behind our project.
    As I worked on the introduction, I focused on explaining the significance of early cardiovascular disease detection, the role of electrocardiogram (ECG) signals in identifying cardiac abnormalities, and how recent advances in artificial intelligence and deep learning have created new opportunities for improving diagnostic accuracy. Writing this section required more than simply summarizing sources—it involved connecting ideas from multiple research papers into a cohesive narrative that naturally leads to our research objectives. This process helped me better understand how a strong introduction provides readers with both the necessary context and a clear rationale for the work being presented.
