what_i_learned: |
  Today's session was fully dedicated to research lab work, and it turned out to be a genuinely productive day. With the help of my graduate mentor and one of my teammates, I was able to make real headway reviewing my code from yesterday's debugging session.
  The key fix was expanding my perturbations to handle a larger number of data samples, which my implementation was not originally set up to do. This adjustment is important for reproducibility and turned out to be the root cause of my earlier issues. Working with smaller datasets had been producing scores outside the required thresholds for key metrics like Energy Drift Correlation (EDC) and CFD Reliability Gain (CRG). Identifying and resolving this gives me much more confidence that I can move smoothly through the remaining experiments required for the research.
  During the session, my team played a fun word-association style game where we went around naming keywords related to our research. As part of the game, we also defined some of the abbreviations we use regularly, which was a great way to make sure everyone on the team has a solid, shared understanding of the terminology behind our work.

blockers: |
  My main blocker heading into today was the perturbation pipeline issue from my BIDMC loader that I identified yesterday. I was able to resolve this today by expanding the perturbations to properly handle a larger number of data samples, which fixed the threshold issues I was seeing with EDC and CRG scores. I now feel confident moving forward with the rest of the experiments.
