what_i_learned: |
  Today was another well-rounded day that balanced deep technical work with some genuinely inspiring sessions. The morning started with the research lab where I continued developing a robust perturbation engine designed to simulate both benign biological variations and non-benign cases in alignment with our research standards. A key focus right now is refining the perturbations to ensure that the benign and non-benign clusters exhibit the natural overlap that would be expected in real clinical data — which is a more nuanced challenge than it might initially seem.
  Following the research session, we had an engaging talk with Dr. Shaun Gittens on the concept of surfing the exponential. He covered several forward-looking ideas including the shift in the developer interface from traditional IDEs to intent-driven programming, agentic programming as a continuous loop rather than a one-time prompt, and the concept of harness engineering. He also shared practical ways to empower ourselves and grow our skillsets, including participating in hackathons, summer coding programs, bootcamps, senior agentic capstone projects, and taking on bigger, more ambitious projects overall. It was a thought-provoking session that pushed me to think more expansively about where the field is heading.

  After that, Dr. Mack introduced us to the Overleaf template we will be using to write our research paper and walked us through the basics of how to navigate and use the platform. It was helpful to get a concrete sense of the structure our paper will follow as we move deeper into the research phase.
  In the afternoon, my teammates and I worked on our weekly slide showcase, and our team went first in the presentations. It honestly felt really good to present early and then sit back and watch the other teams without the anxiety of waiting. Listening to the progress and challenges other teams encountered throughout the week was insightful and gave me a broader perspective on the range of research being done across the program.

blockers: |
  No major blockers today. The perturbation engine work is ongoing and the refinement process is taking careful attention, particularly around achieving the right degree of natural overlap between the benign and non-benign clusters. This is expected to continue into next week as I fine-tune the implementation.

reflection: |
  Today struck a great balance between heads-down technical work and broader intellectual inspiration. Dr. Gittens' session on surfing the exponential was one of the more thought-provoking talks of the program so far — the framing of agentic programming as a loop rather than a prompt particularly resonated with me given the research work I have been doing this week.

  Presenting first during the weekly showcase was an unexpectedly freeing experience. Getting our work out there early meant I could genuinely enjoy listening to the other teams rather than mentally rehearsing our own presentation. Heading into the long weekend feeling productive and energized — and very ready for the break!
