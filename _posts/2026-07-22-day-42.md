what_i_learned: |
  Today, I worked on editing some slides for our final research presentation. I reviewed the content, corrected parts that needed improvement, and helped ensure that the slides clearly communicated the work our team has completed throughout the research program.
  Our graduate mentor also allowed us to divide the responsibility of presenting the slides among the members of our team. Assigning specific slides to each person will help us prepare more effectively because everyone can focus on understanding and explaining their individual sections.
  After the responsibilities were divided, I began familiarizing myself with the slides I will be presenting. I reviewed the information, considered how I would explain it to the audience, and started thinking about how my section connects to the overall presentation.
  I learned that preparing a strong presentation involves more than creating visually appealing slides. It also requires understanding the content, practicing how to communicate it clearly, and coordinating with teammates so that the presentation feels organized and connected.

blockers: |
  One challenge is making sure that I fully understand every detail included in my assigned slides. It is important that I can explain the information confidently instead of simply reading directly from the presentation.
  Our team must also ensure that the transitions between speakers are smooth and that each person stays within the assigned presentation time. We will need to practice together and provide feedback to one another before the final symposium.

reflection: |
  Today made the final symposium feel much closer. Editing the slides and receiving my presentation responsibilities helped me understand what I need to focus on during the remaining preparation period.
  I am looking forward to the final symposium, and I hope my team and I will be able to clearly share everything we have accomplished over the past several weeks. We have invested a significant amount of time in researching, collaborating, and developing our project, so the presentation will be an opportunity to demonstrate the progress we have made.
  I also recognize that presenting our research successfully will require preparation, teamwork, and confidence. I plan to continue reviewing my slides, practicing my explanation, and working with my teammates so that we can deliver a clear and professional final presentation.
