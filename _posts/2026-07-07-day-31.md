what_i_learned: |
    Today's research lab was dedicated to completing the first draft of the introduction section for our research paper. After building the foundation during the previous lab, I focused on refining the organization of the content, improving transitions between ideas, and ensuring that the motivation behind our research was communicated clearly. By the end of the session, I successfully completed the first draft of the introduction, providing a solid starting point for future revisions and team feedback.
    After finishing the introduction, I continued searching for peer-reviewed research papers related to our research topic. My goal was to identify credible sources that not only strengthened the claims made throughout the introduction but also contributed to the references section of our research paper. I reviewed papers covering early cardiovascular disease detection, electrocardiogram (ECG) signal analysis, artificial intelligence, and deep learning techniques, carefully selecting studies that were relevant to our project's objectives. This process gave me a greater appreciation for the importance of conducting a comprehensive literature review and building a strong collection of references before moving further into the writing process.

blockers: |
    I did not encounter any blockers during today's research lab. The writing process progressed smoothly, and I was able to complete my assigned tasks while continuing to expand our collection of relevant research papers. Having a clear objective for the session allowed me to stay productive and make steady progress on both the introduction and the supporting literature.

reflection: |
    Completing the first draft of the introduction feels like a significant milestone in the development of our research paper. It is rewarding to see the ideas and background research we've been gathering evolve into a cohesive narrative that clearly introduces the problem, establishes the importance of our work, and prepares readers for the rest of the paper.
    Continuing to search for relevant research papers also reminded me that academic research is an ongoing process. Finding reliable sources, evaluating their contributions, and integrating them into our work is just as important as writing the paper itself. Each strong reference strengthens the credibility of our research and provides a solid foundation for the arguments we present. I'm looking forward to receiving feedback from my teammates and faculty mentor so we can continue refining the paper and building an even stronger final submission.
