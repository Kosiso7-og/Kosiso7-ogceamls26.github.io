what_i_learned: |
  Today began in the research lab, where I continued reviewing and practicing the slides I will present during the final symposium. Going over my section again helped me become more familiar with the content and identify areas where I still need to improve my explanation.
  Later, we visited the hall where the final symposium will take place. Seeing the presentation space helped me better understand what to expect on Sunday and made the event feel more real.
  One of the program coordinators also explained the schedule, activities, and timelines for the events happening throughout the symposium weekend. This gave us a clearer understanding of where we need to be, what will happen during each part of the weekend, and how we should prepare.
  I learned that becoming familiar with both the presentation content and the environment can help reduce nervousness and improve confidence before an important event.

blockers: |
  My main challenge is continuing to improve my confidence and delivery before the final presentation. Although I have become more familiar with my slides, I still need to practice explaining the information naturally and clearly without relying on the slides.
  I also need to make sure that I understand the timing and transitions between the members of my team. Before Sunday, I plan to continue practicing my section, reviewing the visualizations, and making sure I can clearly answer possible questions from the audience.

reflection: |
  After lunch, I went to the field for field day. I really enjoyed participating in all the activities, and I was surprised by how competitive I became. My favorite activity was the flag game, and my team won, which made the experience even more exciting.
  Field day was a fun opportunity to take a break from our research and presentation preparation. It allowed us to relax, work together in a different setting, and enjoy spending time with one another outside the lab.
  Overall, today was a good and enjoyable day. I was able to continue preparing for the final symposium while also participating in a fun program activity. I am looking forward to presenting on Sunday and sharing the work my team and I have completed throughout the research program.
