what_i_learned: |
  Today began in the research lab, where I practiced walking through the slides I will present during the final symposium. About 40 minutes into the day, it was my team's turn to present our slides as part of our preparation for the symposium.
  The presentation went fairly well, but it also revealed several areas where I can improve. The program coordinators shared helpful feedback and corrections that I can use to make my explanations clearer and strengthen my overall delivery.
  After the presentation, I spent time preparing a script for my assigned slides. I also reviewed the slides more carefully and improved my understanding of some of the visualizations I will need to explain.
  I learned that practicing in front of others is an important part of preparing for a presentation. It helps reveal areas that may seem clear while reviewing alone but require additional explanation when speaking to an audience.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was explaining some of the visualizations clearly and confidently. Although I understood the general purpose of the graphs, I realized that I needed a deeper understanding of the details so that I could explain them in a simple and accurate way.
  I also need to improve my delivery by reducing my dependence on the slides and making my presentation sound more natural. Preparing a script will help organize my thoughts, but I will also need to practice enough that I do not sound as though I am simply reading from it.
  Before the final symposium, I plan to review the feedback from the coordinators, revise my script, and continue practicing my section.

reflection: |
  Today's practice presentation was a valuable opportunity to understand what I am doing well and what still needs improvement. Although the presentation was not perfect, I am glad that we had the chance to receive feedback before the final symposium.
  After working on our presentation, we spent the rest of the day tie-dyeing shirts for the field day happening tomorrow. It was a fun and relaxing activity after spending so much time preparing our research presentation. The last time I made a tie-dye shirt was when I was in junior school, which was almost a decade ago, so the activity brought back good memories.
  I am looking forward to field day tomorrow and to spending time with the other participants outside the research lab. I am also looking forward to the final symposium on Sunday. I hope to use the remaining time to strengthen my understanding of my slides, improve my delivery, and help my team present our work clearly and confidently.
