what_i_learned: |
  Today was my first full day after completing the research program. I spent most of the day doing laundry, organizing my belongings, and packing my boxes in preparation for leaving.
  I also went to a pawn shop to see whether I could sell the refrigerator I purchased during the program. Preparing to move reminded me that completing a summer experience involves more than finishing the academic work. It also requires organizing, planning, and making sure everything is ready for the transition back home.
  Later, we celebrated Ayomide's birthday with her and had some cake. It was a fun way to spend time together before everyone begins leaving and returning to their different schools and homes.
  Today taught me that endings can be both exciting and emotional. I am happy to have completed the program successfully, but I am also beginning to realize how much I will miss the people, places, and experiences that became part of my routine this summer.

blockers: |

  Another challenge was processing the mixed emotions that came with preparing to leave. I am excited to return to Nashville, see my college friends, and be back on campus, but saying goodbye to the people and environment I became familiar with is not easy.

reflection: |
  I am officially done with the research program, and I feel proud and grateful. This experience gave me the opportunity to gain new knowledge, strengthen my research and presentation skills, build meaningful connections, and meet people who made the summer memorable.
  I am very excited to return to Nashville, reconnect with my college friends, and be back on Fisk's campus. At the same time, I will definitely miss Morgan's beautiful campus, the friends I made, the people I interacted with, and the community that welcomed me throughout the program.
  Celebrating Ayomide's birthday was a joyful part of the day and reminded me of the friendships that developed during the summer. Although this chapter is ending, I hope to maintain the connections I made and carry everything I learned into my final year of college and future opportunities.
  Overall, today was fun, productive, and emotional. I am leaving this program with gratitude for the experience and excitement for what comes next.
