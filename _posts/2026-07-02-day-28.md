    Today's research lab session marked an important milestone in our summer research project. I successfully completed all of the experiments outlined in the research manual that my group has been following throughout the project. Reaching this point gave me a much clearer understanding of the complete experimental workflow, from configuring each experiment to interpreting and validating the resulting outputs.
    After finishing the experiments, I focused on documenting my results and observations. I organized my outputs, summarized key findings, and uploaded everything into our shared Google document so that the rest of my group and our faculty mentor can review the work. This process reinforced the importance of clear technical documentation in collaborative research. Producing accurate records is just as valuable as running the experiments themselves, since it enables teammates to reproduce results, compare observations, and identify areas for further discussion or improvement.
