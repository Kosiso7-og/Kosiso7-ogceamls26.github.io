what_i_learned: |
  Today, I took an unexcused absence from the research lab because I needed to complete an online assessment as part of an interview process for a role I'm recruiting for. I spent the earlier part of the day preparing by reviewing relevant concepts, practicing potential questions, and making sure I was ready to complete the assessment under the required conditions.
  After preparing, I completed the online assessment sometime before 5:00 p.m. The experience reminded me of the importance of managing multiple academic and professional responsibilities, especially when important opportunities overlap with existing commitments.
  Although I was unable to participate in the lab activities today, I learned that staying accountable also means taking the initiative to communicate with my teammates, understand what was completed during my absence, and determine what responsibilities still require my attention.

blockers: |
  The main challenge today was balancing the online assessment with my research responsibilities. Preparing for and completing the assessment required most of my attention, which prevented me from attending the research session and contributing to the team's work during the day.
  Because I was absent, I do not yet have complete information about the progress made by my teammates or any new tasks that may have been assigned. I will need to reach out to them, review any shared notes, code, or updates, and identify the portion of the work that I am responsible for completing.

reflection: |
  Today was different from my usual research days because my focus was primarily on an important professional opportunity. Completing the online assessment was a necessary step in the interview process, and I am glad that I dedicated enough time to preparing and submitting it before the deadline.
  At the same time, today reminded me that being part of a research team requires consistent communication and accountability. Missing one session does not remove my responsibility to remain informed or contribute to the project. I plan to contact my teammates, learn what they accomplished during my absence, and complete the part of the work assigned to me.
  Overall, today reinforced the importance of balancing career development with existing commitments. It also showed me that when conflicts occur, the best response is to communicate, take responsibility, and make a clear plan to catch up.
