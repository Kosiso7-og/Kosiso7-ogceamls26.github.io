what_i_learned: |
  Today, I took my first unexcused absence from the research program because I was experiencing severe cramps and wasn't feeling well enough to participate effectively. Although I wasn't in the lab, I learned an important lesson about recognizing when it's necessary to prioritize my health. Research is a demanding and collaborative process, but taking the time to rest when needed ensures that I can return with the energy and focus needed to contribute meaningfully to my team's work.
  While resting, I also spent some time taking care of personal responsibilities that I had been putting off. Although I wasn't actively working on our research paper, I remained mindful of our project's progress and planned to reconnect with my teammates to learn what had been accomplished during the day. My goal is to review their updates, catch up on any changes to the introduction or literature review, and continue contributing where needed once I return.

blockers: |
  The only blocker today was not feeling physically well enough to attend the research lab. Because I missed the day's activities, I will need to communicate with my teammates to understand the progress that was made and identify any new tasks or revisions assigned to me.

reflection: |
  Although today wasn't productive in the traditional sense, it reminded me that maintaining my health is essential for long-term success. It's easy to feel guilty about taking time away from work, especially during an intensive research program, but I've realized that rest is just as important as productivity. Returning before I've recovered would likely reduce both the quality of my work and my ability to contribute to the team.
  Tomorrow, I plan to reconnect with my teammates, review the progress made in my absence, and quickly get back on track with our research paper. I'm grateful to be part of a collaborative team where everyone contributes toward a common goal, and I'm looking forward to continuing our work with renewed energy after taking the time I needed to recover.
