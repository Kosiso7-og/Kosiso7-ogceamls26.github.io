    Today began with our morning research lab session, where I caught up on the work my team had completed during my absence. They had successfully loaded the MIMIC dataset and begun running our experiments, so I spent the first part of the morning understanding the workflow and reproducing the steps on my own. After loading the dataset successfully, I worked through the preprocessing pipeline and extracted the biosignal domains required for our analysis.
    One of the most rewarding parts of the day was successfully generating all 15 pairwise domain combinations used in our cross-domain feature discovery experiment. From those results, my optimal domain pair was ECG Time-Frequency (ECG_TF) and PPG Time (PPG_Time). Working through this experiment helped me better understand how different representations of ECG and PPG signals can capture complementary physiological information and how domain pairing can improve the analysis of biomedical signals. I also became more comfortable with the preprocessing pipeline, organizing extracted features, and interpreting the experimental outputs.
